## 1. Executive Overview & Selection Policy

In strict accordance with `AGENTS.md` Rules 4, 15, and 23:
1. **Purposeful Ingestion:** SatQuery does not blindly download massive external datasets. Each dataset is vetted for licensing, provenance, sensor fidelity, format diversity, and utility in remote-sensing domain adaptation.
2. **Benchmark Integrity:** Frozen test sets remain strictly disjoint from training data. Parent-scene partitioning is enforced across all spatial crops.
3. **Format Breadth:** Candidates span GeoTIFF, TIFF, PNG, and JPEG to establish end-to-end format robustness.

## 3. Provenance & Benchmark Discipline Rules

1. **Frozen Test Exclusion:** The 13 test scenes (`P1225`, `P2912`, `P2982`, `P4055`, `P4265`, `P4627`, `levir_val_18..20`, `diagnostic_agriculture`, `diverse_agriculture_01`, `diverse_nuisance_registration_01`, `diverse_water_01`) remain completely excluded from training.
2. **Format Augmentation Rule:** When augmenting RGB pairs into PNG, JPEG, or TIFF representations, both $T_1$ and $T_2$ receive symmetric processing to prevent synthetic compression artifacts from masquerading as physical surface changes.
3. **Hardware Constraint:** All models must run cleanly under $\le 12$ GB VRAM on the NVIDIA RTX 4070 SUPER.
