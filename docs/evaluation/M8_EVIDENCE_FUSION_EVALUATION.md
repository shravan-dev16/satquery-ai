## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

Milestone M8 establishes the evidence-first reliability layer for SatQuery AI. Positioned directly between specialist model execution (M1–M7) and final system-level confidence estimation (M9), M8 accomplishes three foundational engineering objectives:

1. **Evidence Normalization & Provenance Preservation:** Ingests heterogeneous outputs from single-image, bi-temporal, and cross-modal specialist models and standardizes them into structured `EvidenceItem` records with uncorrupted spatial, temporal, radiometric, and model provenance.
2. **Deterministic Multi-Source Consistency Checking:** Cross-evaluates independent evidence streams across 8 deterministic consistency rules (C1–C8) to identify contradictions, unsupported spatial references, area mismatches, temporal direction inversions, and sensor disagreements.
3. **Evidence Gating & Answer Protection:** Protects answer generation by gating claims according to an auditable `ConsistencyReport` (`CONSISTENT`, `PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT`, `UNCERTAIN`, `CONTRADICTORY`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`), ensuring unverified, ambiguous, or contradictory statements are never presented to remote-sensing analysts as verified facts.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Architectural Boundary Decoupling:**
> - **M7:** Exclusively owns query interpretation, routing, and multi-stage DAG planning.
> - **M8:** Owns evidence normalization, provenance preservation, consistency rules C1–C8, evidence gating, and qualitative reliability reporting.
> - **M9:** Exclusively owns final system-level confidence calculation and probability calibration. M8 does NOT calculate final system confidence.
> - **M10:** Exclusively owns remote-sensing foundation model adaptation (BigEarthNet / LoRA).

## 2. Evidence Architecture & Data Contracts

### 2.1 Standardized Evidence Unit (`EvidenceItem`)
Every factual claim generated by SatQuery must be traceable to a discrete `EvidenceItem`:

```python
class EvidenceItem(BaseModel):
    item_id: str                          # Unique identifier (e.g. 'box_0a1b2c3d')
    source_specialist: str                # Specialist (e.g. 'CHANGE_DETECT', 'CHANGE_VQA', 'RS_GROUND')
    evidence_type: str                    # Type: spatial_mask, zonal_statistics, semantic_transition, bounding_box, etc.
    modality: ModalityType                # OPTICAL, SAR, BITEMPORAL, CROSS_MODAL
    claim: str                            # Factual assertion of the evidence unit
    region_id: Optional[str]              # Linked spatial cluster or region ID
    geometry: Optional[Dict[str, Any]]    # Pixel bounds [xmin, ymin, xmax, ymax], GeoJSON Polygon
    metrics: Dict[str, Any]               # Numeric metrics (changed_pixels, area_ha, ExG, backscatter_dB)
    specialist_confidence: float          # Raw specialist confidence in [0.0, 1.0] (NOT final system confidence)
    is_uncertain: bool                    # Flag indicating ambiguity or low signal
    uncertainty_rationale: Optional[str]  # Explanation for uncertainty
    provenance: Dict[str, Any]            # Audit trace: filenames, CRS, resolution, model checkpoint
```

### 2.2 Consistency Status & Report (`ConsistencyReport`)
The consistency subsystem outputs an auditable status assessment:

```python
class ConsistencyStatus(str, Enum):
    CONSISTENT = "CONSISTENT"
    PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT = "PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT"
    UNCERTAIN = "UNCERTAIN"
    CONTRADICTORY = "CONTRADICTORY"
    INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE = "INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE"
```

The report aggregates:
- `status`: High-level qualitative assessment
- `is_gated`: Boolean indicating whether the final narrative was gated/modified
- `gating_action`: `"allow"`, `"qualify"`, `"flag_contradiction"`, `"state_insufficient"`
- `conflicts`: List of detected `EvidenceConflict` records
- `supporting_evidence_ids`: IDs of corroborated evidence items
- `specialist_confidences`: Map of raw confidences from invoked specialists
- `evidence_quality_score`: Indicator of evidence completeness in $[0.0, 1.0]$
- `summary_narrative`: Concise audit explanation

## 3. The 8 Deterministic Consistency Rules (C1 – C8)

| Rule ID | Name | Trigger Condition | Severity | Resulting Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | **Zero-Change Contradiction** | `CHANGE_DETECT` detects 0 changed pixels, but `CHANGE_VQA` claims active land-cover transition or `temporal_direction != 'no_change'`. | Critical | `CONTRADICTORY` |
| **C2** | **Region Support** | `CHANGE_VQA` references a `region_id` that is not present in spatial detector evidence. | Warning | `PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT` / `UNCERTAIN` |
| **C3** | **Nonzero Change Support** | Semantic transition claims emitted without nonzero spatial change support. | Critical | `CONTRADICTORY` |
| **C4** | **Area Consistency** | Claimed numeric area diverges from measured detector zonal statistics by $>10\%$ tolerance. | Warning | `PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT` |
| **C5** | **Temporal Direction & Evidence** | Chronology: $T_1 \le T_2$ verified. Direction: `increased`/`decreased`/`modified` must be supported by $T_1 \rightarrow T_2$ transition evidence (not chronology alone). | Critical / Warning | `CONTRADICTORY` / `UNCERTAIN` |
| **C6** | **Optical/SAR Agreement** | Cross-modal analysis shows severe divergence between optical reflectance cues and SAR physical backscatter / roughness. | Warning | `PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT` / `UNCERTAIN` |
| **C7** | **Class / Transition Conflict** | Implausible transition asserted without physical feature backing (e.g. water to high-rise building with 10 pixels). | Warning | `PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT` |
| **C8** | **Evidence Sufficiency** | Empty candidate detection for explicit referring query, degenerate boxes ($\ge 98\%$ area), or missing CRS for physical queries. | Warning | `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` |

## 4. Evidence Gating Policy

To guarantee scientific credibility, `EvidenceGater` enforces four gating policies on the final answer:

1. **`CONSISTENT` $\rightarrow$ Allow:** The specialist answer narrative is verified as factually corroborated and returned directly.
2. **`PARTIALLY_CONSISTENT` / `UNCERTAIN` $\rightarrow$ Qualify:** The answer is appended with an explicit advisory notice citing identified discrepancies (e.g. `"[Note: Partially consistent; 1 conflict noted]"`).
3. **`CONTRADICTORY` $\rightarrow$ Flag Contradiction:** Conflicting claims are blocked from presentation as fact. The answer explicitly surfaces the contradiction:
   > `"[CONTRADICTION DETECTED] Spatial change detector verified 0 changed pixels, but semantic VQA asserted active transition ('bare_land -> built_up'). The semantic interpretation cannot be verified as factual because it directly contradicts underlying physical spatial evidence."`
4. **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` $\rightarrow$ Declare Insufficiency:** The system explicitly acknowledges that observational evidence is insufficient rather than guessing:
   > `"[INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE] No spatial candidate regions satisfied detection confidence thresholds for 'airport runway'. A definitive analytical conclusion cannot be grounded without additional observational data."`

## 5. End-to-End Workflow Case Studies

### 5.1 Case A: Fully Consistent Bi-Temporal Change Workflow
- **Query:** *"What changed between these two dates?"*
- **Inputs:** High-resolution optical pairs $T_1$ and $T_2$ (LEVIR-CD urban expansion sample).
- **Specialists:** `CHANGE_DETECT` (TinyCD) $\rightarrow$ `CHANGE_VQA` (Conditioned Qwen2-VL).
- **Evidence Produced:**
  - `CHANGE_DETECT`: 9,102 changed pixels ($3.47\%$ AOI), 2 spatial clusters (`cluster_001`, `cluster_002`), physical area $9.10\text{ ha}$.
  - `CHANGE_VQA`: Direction `modified`, predominant transition `bare_land -> residential_construction`, referencing `cluster_001`.
- **M8 Assessment:**
  - Rule C1: Passed ($9102 > 0$).
  - Rule C2: Passed (`cluster_001` exists).
  - Rule C4: Passed (area aligns).
  - Rule C5: Passed ($T_1 < T_2$, direction supported by construction evidence).
- **Consistency Status:** **`CONSISTENT`** (`evidence_quality_score: 1.0`).
- **Trace Steps:**
  `InputValidation` $\rightarrow$ `TemporalValidation` $\rightarrow$ `SpatialCompatibility` $\rightarrow$ `Alignment` $\rightarrow$ `SpecialistSelection` $\rightarrow$ `ModelExecution` $\rightarrow$ `ChangeMaskValidation` $\rightarrow$ `Statistics` $\rightarrow$ `SemanticInterpretation` $\rightarrow$ `EvidenceNormalization` $\rightarrow$ `ConsistencyCheck` $\rightarrow$ `EvidenceFusion` $\rightarrow$ `EvidenceAssembly` (13 steps).

### 5.2 Case B: Deliberate Contradiction (Anti-Hallucination Gate)
- **Query:** *"Describe new construction between these two dates."*
- **Inputs:** Identical optical images ($T_1 == T_2$).
- **Specialists:** `CHANGE_DETECT` $\rightarrow$ `CHANGE_VQA` (adversarial simulated response).
- **Evidence Produced:**
  - `CHANGE_DETECT`: 0 changed pixels, 0 clusters.
  - `CHANGE_VQA`: Asserted *"Large new industrial warehouse built in the center."*
- **M8 Assessment:**
  - Rule C1: **VIOLATED** (`C1_ZERO_CHANGE_CONTRADICTION`, critical).
  - Rule C3: **VIOLATED** (`C3_NONZERO_CHANGE_SUPPORT`, critical).
- **Consistency Status:** **`CONTRADICTORY`** (`evidence_quality_score: 0.20`, `gating_action: "flag_contradiction"`).
- **Final Gated Answer:** Corrected automatically to flag contradiction; hallucinations suppressed.

### 5.3 Case C: Single-Image Grounding Insufficiency
- **Query:** *"Where is the submarine base?"*
- **Inputs:** Single agricultural GeoTIFF.
- **Specialist:** `RS_GROUND` (Grounding DINO).
- **Evidence Produced:** 0 detected boxes above threshold ($0.35$).
- **M8 Assessment:**
  - Rule C8: **VIOLATED** (`C8_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`).
  - No manufactured cross-model consensus.
- **Consistency Status:** **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`** (`gating_action: "state_insufficient"`).
- **Final Gated Answer:** System explicitly states insufficient evidence to localize requested feature.

## 7. Known Limitations & M9 Transition

1. **Deterministic Rule Heuristics:** Consistency checking is rule-based and deterministic. While this prevents ungrounded hallucinations, complex contextual edge cases (e.g. gradual wetland drying vs. seasonal draw-down) require multi-year time series rather than single pairs.
2. **Confidence Independence:** In accordance with the user's architectural corrections, M8 exposes qualitative factors and structured conflict items only. Numerical confidence scoring and probability calibration are strictly deferred to **Milestone M9**.
